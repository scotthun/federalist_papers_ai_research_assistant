What does Madison argue about factions in Federalist No. 10?
Madison argues that factions are sown into the nature of liberty itself — to remove them would be to abolish freedom, "a remedy worse than the disease." Instead, a large republic controls their effects by diluting factional influence across many representatives and interests[1]
